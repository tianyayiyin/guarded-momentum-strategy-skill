Guarded Momentum Strategy Skill is a risk-aware strategy layer for autonomous trading systems. It reads CMC-style OHLCV and sentiment data, then outputs a structured recommendation: BUY, HOLD, or REDUCE, with confidence, max position size, stop-loss, take-profit, diagnostics, and plain-English rationale.
